THE CORE: Understanding Kernel Architecture

The unseen engine powering every device you own.

What is a Kernel?

The kernel is the main control center of an operating system. It talks to your hardware and your apps. Phones, laptops, consoles, and ATMs rely on it.

Think of a busy restaurant. The customers are apps. The kitchen is hardware. The manager listens to orders, coordinates workers, prevents accidents, and makes sure food is served. That manager is the kernel.

The Three Kernel Architectures

Monolithic Kernel

Everything is inside one big block. It is fast since parts talk directly. If one part breaks, the whole system can crash. Example: Linux

Single Big Core

Microkernel

Only basics stay in the center. Other services live around it. More stable and safer but sometimes slower due to extra communication. Example: Minix

Small Core + Services

Hybrid Kernel

Mix of both styles. Some parts are grouped for speed. Others stay separate for safety. Examples: Windows NT, macOS
